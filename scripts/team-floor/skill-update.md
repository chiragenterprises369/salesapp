# Vistar Live Team Floor (SPEC 12 and 12.1)

Contract: `TeamFloor`, `TeamFloorFlat` and `TeamFloorProps` in `apps/web/features/team-floor/index.tsx`;
snapshot `TeamFloorSnapshotSchema` (`schemas/dashboard.ts`), events in `events.ts`, palettes in
`design/tokens.ts`. Read `vistar-theming` and `vistar-realtime-events` too. Versions: `three` 0.187,
`@react-three/fiber` 9, `@react-three/drei` 10 (context7 for docs). Pipeline commands, timings and
QA scripts: `scripts/team-floor/README.md`. Licenses: `docs/ASSETS.md`.

## Architecture
- `index.tsx` stays light: mode decision, KPI strip, panels, flat plan. `next/dynamic(() => import('./scene/floor-scene'), { ssr: false })` loads three/R3F. Check with `node e2e/team-floor/bundle-check.mjs` (no three in the initial chunks).
- Data: one pure reducer (`state/reducer.ts`) from the snapshot plus `DomainEvent`s; Wave 1 feeds `mockTeamFloorSnapshot()` + `createEventSimulator()`, Wave 3 the socket, unchanged.
- `scene/engine.ts` (`FloorEngine`) owns every three.js object and the label positions; React state changes only on events. Per-frame work never goes through React.
- Folders: `state/` (template schema, layout, seats, pathfinding, reducer), `scene/` (engine, office, glow, characters, agents, effects, camera, labels, look), `flat/` (SVG plan, accessible list), `panel/`.

## Floor templates (layout as data)
- `public/team-floor/templates/<small|medium|large>.json`, generated by `scripts/team-floor/templates/build-templates.mjs` and validated by `FloorTemplateSchema` (zod). The Blender build reads the same JSON, so geometry and runtime agree.
- Contents: footprint, walls (back = tall inner face, front = low outer face, glass, glass_walnut, partition; door and window openings), rooms (floor material), seats (desk, booth, cabin, reception, lounge, hot_desk; position, facing, work surface, screen id, call facing), standing spots, hub, entrance, walking graph (nodes + line-of-sight edges), props, lamps, obstacles, camera.
- Choice by head count: <= 8 Small, <= 20 Medium, otherwise Large (`templateFor`); `?floorTemplate=` overrides (QA only).
- Medium is hand-composed after the reference render. Small and Large are composed from the module kit (`templates/modules.mjs`, D-054): `sales_pod` (4 or 6 desks), `calling_room` (N booths), `manager_cabin`, `lounge`, `reception`, `hub_plaza`, `print_corner`, `hot_bench`. Placements are stored in the template (`modules`) and the catalogue in `templates/modules.json`; 28.3 extends this kit.
- One person per desk, never a dense wall of desks: 1.4 m aisles between pod rows, 1.8 m between pods.
- Homes: cabin to the most senior person, reception to a viewer, `deskIndex` desks, then hot desks, standing spots, overflow (`state/seats.ts`).

## Office art (Blender, scripted)
- `scripts/team-floor/blender/build_office.py --template <id> --bake --export` builds the office from the template, culls faces the fixed camera never sees, unwraps lightmap UVs, bakes Day, Evening (diffuse irradiance) and AO in Cycles, exports a Draco GLB with WebP textures. `bake-all.ps1` runs all templates.
- GLB contract: `office_static` (lightmapped, UV1), `office_glass`, `office_emissive`, `screen:<seatId>`, `hub_screen`, `inst:<key>:<n>` (repeated props -> InstancedMesh).
- Lightmaps: `office-<id>-day.webp`, `-evening.webp`, `-ao.webp`, texel = srgb(irradiance / scale); scale per map in `office-<id>.bake.json`. Budgets: office GLB <= 4 MB, characters <= 2 MB, lightmaps <= 2 MB per theme.
- Never commit `.blend` files or anything under `scripts/team-floor/.out/`.

## Look and theme
- Static office: unlit `MeshBasicMaterial` x lightmap x AO (cheapest and closest to the bake). Day and Evening maps are blended in the shader by `lightMix` (shared uniforms); a theme switch cross-fades over 400 ms (`THEME_TWEEN_S`), instantly with reduced motion.
- Light = Day bake (warm sun from the upper left, soft sky). Dark = Evening bake (warm downlight pools, cool window light, glowing monitors, deep navy). Without an Evening map the dark look dims the Day map (`interimTint`).
- Restrained bloom = glow cards on emissive sources only (`scene/glow.ts`: lamps, screens, hub ring, reception edge), Evening only, off on the low tier.
- Characters and instanced props use realtime hemisphere + key + camera-side fill; characters get a soft rim (stronger in Evening) and a ~1 px inverted-hull outline.
- UI accents from `palettes[theme]`; office art colours are authored in Blender (`props.py`, mirrored in `look.ts` ART and `flat/floor-map.tsx` PLAN).

## Characters
- `characters.glb` (`build_characters.py`): one armature, two builds x two sleeve lengths, hair per `AvatarConfig.hairStyle`, phone and document props, clips idle, sit, typing, phone_sit, phone_stand, walk, walk_carry, wave, wave_sit, sleep_sit, receive_sit. Slots skin, hair, top, bottom, shoes, eyes, accent are tinted from `AvatarConfig`. Above 80 people the procedural instanced figures are used.
- Drawn at 1.12x (heroic scale, like the reference); seated clips put the pelvis on the 0.47 m seat.

## Event mapping
| Event | Floor reaction |
| --- | --- |
| `employee.status_changed` | on_call: booth (or desk), phone clip, blue ring; busy: desk, typing; idle: desk or wave; away: lounge; offline: asleep at the desk or an empty chair |
| `lead.created` | glowing lead card at the Lead Hub, waiting count on the hub screen |
| `lead.assigned` | card flies to the owner's desk; owner turns and receives it |
| `call.started` / `call.ended` | rings and timer; outcome bubble (answered, missed, interested) for ~3 s |
| `task.overdue`, `attention.raised` | clickable "Needs you" bubble above the person (or the cabin); `attention.resolved` removes it |

## Movement
- A* on the template walking graph (`state/pathfinding.ts`), string pulling and Catmull-Rom smoothing, cached routes; seats and spots attach to their `approach` node.

## Labels and camera
- DOM labels placed by `scene/label-layout.ts`: bubbles in their own lane first, then tags by priority (selected, needs you, on call, others), leader lines when moved, compact dot + initials when crowded (and from the start for low-priority tags when more than 20 people are on screen at the fit zoom), hidden last. Zero overlaps in the default view (`e2e/team-floor/labels.spec.ts`).
- Fixed isometric orthographic camera from the template (yaw 45, pitch 27), auto-fit, eased zoom and pan limits, click-to-focus, Fit button, keyboard pan and zoom on the canvas region.

## Performance
- Instanced props, merged static meshes, `frameloop="demand"` with a capped ambient rate, paused when hidden. Tiers high | medium | low from GPU detection and an FPS monitor (drop after 3 s under 45 fps). Low = no shadows, DPR 1, no glow, no outlines.
- Target 60 fps with 30 people on a mid laptop (`node e2e/team-floor/fps.mjs <base> 30 10 medium`).

## Modes and accessibility
- 3D | Flat toggle (`teamFloorMode`); `auto` = flat on coarse pointer or small screens, reduced motion, low tier or no WebGL.
- Flat view: `flat/floor-map.tsx` draws the same template as an SVG plan (room colours, furniture, seat dots, status rings, needs-you badges) for every template; the accessible employee list below it is the keyboard and screen-reader path (one tab stop, arrow keys, Enter opens the panel).
- The accessible list is always rendered (visually hidden next to the 3D canvas, shown while focused). KPI strip, Needs-you panel and the employee side panel are shared by both modes; `readOnly` hides actions.
